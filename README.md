# 🥔 End-to-End Potato Disease Classification using CNN  

![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/python-3.8%2B-blue)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-green)

An end‑to‑end pipeline designed to classify potato leaf diseases using **Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)**, providing actionable insights for agriculture and plant health monitoring.  

---

## 🌱 Why It Matters
- Early detection of potato leaf diseases improves crop yield  
- Helps farmers reduce losses by identifying blight quickly  
- Supports agricultural research and plant pathology studies  
- Automates disease classification for scalability and efficiency  

---

## 🔑 Workflow
1. Data Collection  
2. Data Cleaning → remove null, empty, incorrect values  
3. Model Building (CNN: input, hidden, output layers)  
4. Performance Analysis → accuracy, precision, F1 score, confusion matrix, z‑score  
5. Testing → validate with new unseen instances  

---

## 🌿 Disease Categories
- Healthy  
- Early Blight  
- Late Blight  

---

## 📂 Data Handling
- Healthy leaves are moved to a separate folder  
- New images replace older ones over time to reflect leaf growth  

---

## 💡 Concept
Leaves often lose their shiny green color due to a **lack of chlorophyll**. Most potato leaf diseases are caused by **pathogens** that disrupt photosynthesis, harming plant growth and productivity.  

---
